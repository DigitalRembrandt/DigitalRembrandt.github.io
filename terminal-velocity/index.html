Terminal Velocity

Buckle up and punch it into hyperspace.

A high-speed 3D endless runner soaked in the neon glow of classic 80s arcade aesthetics. Dodge, weave, and blast your way to a new high score.

Everything you need to go faster.

🚀

Three Playable Ships

Choose your ride: the wide-bodied Raider with swept wings and twin engines, the iconic wireframe Classic pyramid ship, or the sleek neon Light Cycle with a glowing trail.

⚡

Five Power-Ups

Auto-Fire lasers, a Shield that absorbs hits, a Magnet that pulls orbs to you, Slow Mo that bends time, and Deflector rings that destroy everything they touch.

🛣️

Dynamic Grid Road

The neon grid banks, crests hills, and drops into valleys in real time. Obstacles ride the surface with it. No two runs ever feel the same.

💨

Afterburner Speed

Touch the top of the screen to kick in the afterburners and hit up to 5 times normal speed. Stars stretch from points into hyperspace streaks around you.

🎨

Three Visual Modes

Cycle through a slowly shifting full-spectrum color gradient, a classic neon palette of hot pink, cyan, and green, or pure phosphor green on black for authentic retro CRT vibes.

🎵

Original Soundtrack

Ten original music tracks with seamless crossfading and adjustable volume. Designed to keep you in the zone as the speed climbs and the stakes rise.

Your privacy, by design.

Terminal Velocity collects zero personal data. No analytics, no ads, no tracking. Ever. Your high score stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere.
