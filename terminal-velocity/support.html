Frequently asked questions

Is Terminal Velocity free?

Please check the App Store listing for current pricing. We may offer a free version, a paid version, or optional in-app purchases. Check the listing for the most up-to-date information.

How do I unlock the other ships?

The Raider, Classic, and Light Cycle ships are selectable from the ship picker on the main menu. Check the App Store listing for details on how each ship is unlocked.

What does the afterburner do?

Touch the top portion of the screen to engage the afterburner. This ramps the game speed up to 5 times the current pace. Stars stretch into hyperspace streaks around you. Release to drop back to normal speed.

What are Teleport Zones?

Teleport Zones are sections of the grid where ghost obstacles materialise from nowhere. If you survive long enough inside the zone without being hit, you are warped forward on the grid, skipping a stretch of road entirely.

How do I change the visual mode?

The visual mode (Grad, Color, or Mono) can be changed from the Settings screen inside the app. Mono mode renders the game in pure phosphor green on black for a classic CRT look.

My high score was lost after reinstalling. Why?

Your high score is stored locally on your device and is not backed up to the cloud. Deleting or reinstalling the app will reset it. iCloud backup may preserve some data depending on your device settings.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Use the contact form below. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a description of what happened. We read every message.
