Sunset coastlines, storm-soaked highways, and rivals right on your bumper.

Built for the thrill of the drive.

🛣️

Tracks That Never Run Out

Every track is new, with its own bends and straights, and there's always another one ahead. But it's not random: race a track again and it's exactly the same, so you can learn every curve and beat your best time.

🌍

A World Worth the Drive

Golden-hour coastlines and palm-lined straights. Blazing desert, frozen ice roads, misty forests, city streets lined with skyscrapers and gleaming sci-fi skylines, plus building sites where giant port cranes straddle the road.

🌧️

Weather, Day and Night

Race through sunshine, rain, snow and fog, from noon heat to stormy nights. Rain leaves standing water that throws up spray behind every car, and after dark your headlights carve a path through the night.

🏆

Five Rivals, One Championship

Every race is a real fight against five rival drivers. Seasons run eight races deep, every result counts toward the championship, and the table at the end of each season shows exactly where you stand.

🔥

Turbo and Drift

Throw the tail out through the bends and hold the slide, then hit TURBO and watch the world blur as flames pour from the exhausts. Haptics let you feel every curb, bump and crash, all to a soundtrack made for the game.

🚗

Three Cars in the Garage

A thundering muscle car, a low-slung sports car and a nimble coupe. Pick your favorite, or switch between them whenever the mood takes you.

🎮

Play Your Way

Arcade gives you three lives, with health packs on the road to win one back. Cruise is pure, endless driving. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard, put the touch controls at the top or bottom of the screen, or pair a game controller.

🎥

Five Camera Views

Chase the action, pull back for the wide view, drop to the bumper, ride the hood as the horizon rolls with every lean, or go car-free on the road view. Add a rear-view mirror to see who's closing in.

📊

Every Race Remembered

Your racing career, beautifully charted: distance driven, time on track, wins, podiums, clean races, top speeds and your fastest runs. Watch yourself get faster, one race at a time.
